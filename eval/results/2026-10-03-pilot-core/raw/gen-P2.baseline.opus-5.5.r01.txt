External API and events\n\n- REST API with `Idempotency-Key` on all POSTs, cursor pagination and expandable objects.\n- Outbound webhooks (`invoice.paid`, `subscription.updated`, `usage.threshold_reached`) are:\n  - HMAC-signed\n  - retried with backoff\n  - visible in a per-endpoint delivery log, with replay\n- Use a **transactional outbox** pattern: write the domain change and the event in the same Postgres transaction, and a relay publishes to Kafka. Use strong consistency in Postgres, and use sagas with compensating actions across services rather than distributed transactions.\n- **Replayability:** usage, and therefore invoices, can be recomputed from the raw archive and price versions.
