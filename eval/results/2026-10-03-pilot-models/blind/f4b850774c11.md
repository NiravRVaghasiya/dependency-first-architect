## 10. Deferred

### Features not in this plan (pull-forward conditions):

1. **Advanced proration rules** (mid-month refunds, credit rollover, subscription pause/resume):
   - Deferred to Phase 4 or later.
   - Pull forward if: (a) customer demand > 10% of early cohort, or (b) revenue impact analysis shows ≥ 5% MRR savings from upsell (e.g., annual plans with pause option).
   - Reason: Adds complexity to state machine; V2 latency + isolation validation take priority.

2. **Chargeback and dispute handling** (customer disputes processor charge; system routes to manual review):
   - Deferred to Phase 4+ (currently flagged for human triage, not automated workflow).
   - Pull forward if: (a) dispute rate > 1% of transactions (industry benchmark ~0.5%), or (b) processor SLA includes dispute notification (requires webhook handler upgrade).
   - Reason: Low volume in beta; manual triage acceptable; automation ROI unclear until load is known.

3. **Multi-currency and tax calculation**:
   - Deferred to Phase 5+.
   - Pull forward if: (a) International customer requests (GDPR / VAT, non-USD billing), or (b) Tax API integration (Avalara, TaxJar) becomes contractual requirement.
   - Reason: Assumes single-currency (USD) and single-region initially; adds complexity to invoice schema + proration + reconciliation.

4. **In-house payment processing** (no processor integration; direct card settlement):
   - Deliberately out of scope (marked as "N/A — build vs. buy resolved as buy; integration boundary is Phase 2").
   - Rationale: PCI DSS compliance burden (external audit, data handling, liability) outweighs build cost; Stripe handles it better and cheaper.

5. **Real-time settlement** (immediate charge posting, not batch):
   - Deferred to Phase 5+ or business decision.
   - Pull forward if: (a) Customer demand for cash-flow optimization (e.g., SaaS platform wants to settle 3x/day), or (b) Revenue model changes to high-frequency micro-transactions.
   - Reason: Batch settlement (daily or per-transaction) sufficient for Phase 0–5; real-time adds processor complexity and webhook throughput.

6. **Fraud detection and rules engine** (e.g., block repeat chargebacks, flag high-risk transactions):
   - Deferred to Phase 5+.
   - Pull forward if: (a) Fraud rate > 0.5% (industry benchmark) or (b) Customer requirement (high-risk verticals, e.g., gambling, dating apps).
   - Reason: Processor provides native fraud detection (Stripe's default rules); custom rules are nice-to-have.

7. **Invoice customization UI** (customer-facing portal to download/email invoices):
   - Deferred to Phase 5+ (GA or later).
   - Pull forward if: (a) Customer support requests > 5/week (manual inquiries about invoice), or (b) Early-stage SaaS product wants to reduce support burden.
   - Reason: Phase 0–5 assumes invoices are system-generated and emailed by billing system; customer access is deferred.

8. **Dunning and retry workflows** (automatic re-charge for failed cards, escalation emails):
   - Deferred to Phase 5+.
   - Pull forward if: (a) Failed payment rate > 5% (billing system must retry), or (b) Customer demand (high-value accounts need retry logic to minimize churn).
   - Reason: Phase 0–5 assumes charges succeed (or fail permanently); retry logic is a separate system, orthogonal to invoice generation.
