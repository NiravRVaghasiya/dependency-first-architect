Walking skeleton (Phase 0)\n\n**The real request and response:**\n1. **Client 1 types \"Hello\" at position 0** in a shared document; WebSocket sends `{ op: \"insert\", pos: 0, text: \"Hello\", id: \"user1\", clock: 1 }`.\n2. **Server receives, appends to log**, broadcasts to all connected clients.\n3. **Client 2 (joined earlier) receives and applies** the operation to its local state; no durability.\n- **Client 2 (browser):** receives operation, applies to Yjs doc, re-renders.\n\n**Deployment, logging, monitoring, rollback on day zero:**\n- **Deploy:** Single backend container (Node.js + Redis) and client bundle via CDN or static server;
